Concierge Dashboard

A dashboard that helps the concierge team organize and format shift notes, with AI-assisted refinement.

Open interactive demo (new tab)

Overview

I built a dashboard to help the concierge team capture shift notes, organize activity, and keep follow-ups visible. It brings the shift log, team notices, upcoming tasks, and resources into one place, with AI-assisted refinement to help turn rough notes into clearer entries.

The problem

Shift notes need to communicate what happened and what still needs attention. The challenge was to make notes easier to capture and format while giving the team a clear view of activity and follow-ups across shifts.

My contribution

I designed the dashboard around a quick-log entry point and a structured shift activity view. Team filters let users focus on the relevant area, while search makes it possible to look for units, names, notes, and links.

I brought together notices, upcoming tasks, and quick links alongside the log, and included AI-assisted note refinement to support clearer wording and formatting. The focus was on making everyday information easier to enter, find, and review.

The note-writing workflow automatically follows the format set by the company’s standard operating procedures (SOPs). Staff can focus on recording what happened and what needs follow-up, without having to reconstruct the required format each time.

This is especially helpful when the task being documented already involves multiple steps. Formatting the shift note becomes one less thing to remember, making it easier to complete the documentation alongside the operational work and keep handovers consistent.

I also created a public demo so visitors can explore the workflow using fictional data.

Screenshots

Outcome

The dashboard brings note capture, shift activity, and follow-up information into a single interface. The public demo shows how the team can log an update, review activity, and see what needs attention, with AI-assisted refinement supporting the note-writing workflow.

Estimated to reduce shift-note preparation time by approximately 50% through AI-assisted rewriting, formatting, and categorization.

Making notes easier to write also encourages staff to record updates they might otherwise leave undocumented. Lowering that effort can help capture more detail about what happened, what was done, and what still needs attention, supporting more informative handovers.
